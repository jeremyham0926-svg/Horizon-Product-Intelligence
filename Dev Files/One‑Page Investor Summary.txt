🎬 4. Product Demo Script
Use this when showing the product to investors or customers.

Step 1 — Define the Opportunity
User selects industry, segments, and enters a product concept.

Step 2 — Market Intelligence
The system identifies emerging markets, underserved segments, and demand patterns.

Step 3 — Competitor Intelligence
It maps competitors, their strengths, weaknesses, pricing, and positioning.

Step 4 — Product Viability
It evaluates demand, differentiation, feasibility, and profitability.

Step 5 — Constraints
It analyzes regulatory, technical, platform, and operational friction.

Step 6 — Strategic Fit
It aligns the opportunity with the company’s capabilities and goals.

Step 7 — Executive Decision
The system produces a clear recommendation:

“Build Product X. Here’s why it fits your company, why it will win, and how to position it.”
