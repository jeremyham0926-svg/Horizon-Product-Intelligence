📘 MASTER APP SUMMARY — Horizon Product Intelligence
1. App Name
Horizon Product Intelligence

2. Core Purpose
A Python‑based universal product‑opportunity engine that helps companies decide what product they should build next, using structured intelligence across markets, competitors, constraints, internal capabilities, and strategic goals.

3. Target Users
Manufacturing companies

SaaS product teams

Healthcare product innovators

Logistics and supply chain companies

Consumer product companies

Any organization that builds and launches products

4. The Problem It Solves
Companies routinely waste money building the wrong products because:

Market signals are unclear

Competitors move faster

Internal biases distort decisions

Constraints are misunderstood

Capabilities are misaligned

Strategy is reactive instead of predictive

There is no universal system that tells companies what they should build next.

5. The Solution
A Python-driven decision engine that:

Ingests structured company inputs

Runs six intelligence engines

Scores opportunities across multiple dimensions

Synthesizes results into a board‑ready recommendation

Explains why a product fits

Shows how it wins

Identifies risks and constraints

Aligns with executive priorities

6. The Six Intelligence Engines
Market Intelligence Engine

Competitor Intelligence Engine

Product Viability Engine

Constraint Analysis Engine

Strategic Fit Engine

Executive Decision Engine

Each engine produces structured scores and insights.
